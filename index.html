Every carrier shown has active operating authority and an active USDOT number. Pending, inactive and revocation-pending carriers are left out.

Data: FMCSA Company Census, Motus authority history and carrier records, and L&I carrier records on data.transportation.gov. Owner names come from the company officers listed on the MCS-150. FMCSA doesn't record trailer type, so equipment is estimated: box truck means straight trucks and no tractors, power only means tractors and no trailers, and step deck and double drop mean heavy cargo (machinery, construction, oilfield) on a tractor-trailer fleet. Out-of-service orders and insurance aren't in this data, so check them before you book a load.

Lead statuses and notes are saved in this browser only.
